THE PLAN

We're planning the ultimate Alaska vacation — starting in Fairbanks, riding the rails to Denali National Park, continuing on to Anchorage, then boarding the Anthem of the Seas for seven days through Alaska's port cities.

Come for the train, the cruise, or the entire adventure. Read on for the full plan.

PART ONE

Alaska by Land

We'll travel the interior of Alaska aboard the Alaska Wilderness Express — a privately owned, glass-dome rail service that runs the Denali Star route connecting Anchorage, Talkeetna, Denali National Park, and Fairbanks.

There's no group itinerary yet for Fairbanks or Denali — plans are still coming together. Stick with the group when they take shape, or explore on your own.

Upper-Level Dome

Full-length curved glass with 360° panoramic views, and forward-facing reclining leather seats for up to 88 passengers.

Lower-Level Dining

A 36-seat private dining room serving breakfast, lunch, and dinner cooked by an onboard chef. Meals sold separately.

PART TWO

Alaska by Sea

From Seward, we'll board a cruise ship and see the rest of Alaska from the water — sailing past Hubbard Glacier and calling at Juneau, Skagway, Icy Strait Point, and Ketchikan before ending in Vancouver.

Explore each port on your own and book your own excursions, or — if there's enough interest — we'll pull together group outings at the popular stops.

Aboard Anthem of the Seas

A Royal Caribbean ship built for a full week at sea — a North Star observation capsule, indoor skydiving, a FlowRider surf simulator, and a wide mix of restaurants, bars, and live entertainment.
